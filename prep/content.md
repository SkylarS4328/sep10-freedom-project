## Part A: existing technology

##### Software
* The first app that I looked into was **Krita**
  * It helps people who want a free digital art program
  * easy to use on a computer/PC
  * save artwork files directly on computer desktop
  * simple to navigate
  * also supports animations
  * compatible with Windows, Mac OS, and Linux
  * https://krita.org/en/
  * [image of Krita interface](<img width="1366" height="768" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b75664f2-370d-45c7-a091-0262f8ce241d" />
)
* The second app I looked into was **Toon Bloom Harmony**
  * Industry standard animation art program used by big studios and in movies
  * has a monthly subscription service in order to use
  * compatible with Windows and Mac OS
  * 2D hand-drawn animation, and 2D rigging
  * has YouTube channel link on website for tips
  * https://www.toonboom.com/products/harmony
  * [image of some of the studios and companies that have used Toon Bloom Harmony](<img width="1228" height="397" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/75d57822-4e94-48cc-bfe1-e07a007040b5" />
)
* The Third app that I looked into was **Blender**
  * free to use
  * helpful for people who want to do 3D modeling and 3D animation
  * compatible with Windows and Mac OS
  * https://www.blender.org/
