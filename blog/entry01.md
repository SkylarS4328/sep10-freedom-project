# Entry 1 - Choosing a Topic
##### 10/20/26

## Visual Arts
I decided on visual arts as the topic I am going to make my freedom project on because I really enjoy visual arts, I've practiced my art skills for years, ever since I was in middle school. It's something I am extremely passionate about and I think learning about the history of art and how art has evolved due to new technologies can be really enjoyable for me.

## Backup 1: Writing and literature
My second choice was writing because I also enjoy writing stories in my free time and I love reading books, and I think learning about the history of literature and how technology has influenced it would be very facinating.

## Backup 2: Music
My third choice was music. I love listening to music while I do other things and just in general. but I don't know that much about music as I do about writing and art, so I thought it might be something worth exploring and maybe it could help develop an even deeper interest than music and songwriting. 

## Reflection:
I always wanted to choose visual arts as the topic I will be doing this project on, but before, I wasn't sure if it was just because I hadn't explored other possible topics yet. but now I'm sure that this is just what I am most passionate about.
